  40 dB of isolation: the best setting (closed form, *) is 36 dB, SNR 25.57 dB -- 6.00 dB under the best setting without leakage (31.57 dB); there the echo -4.82 dB, the thermal noise -4.08 dB, the phase-noise residue -1.1 dB and the ADC -4.2 dB against it
  the three isolation figures are each 'at most 1 dB over the thermal floor': a noisier setting passes them more easily, which is why the SNR that is left is the figure to read
  what the closed form adds to example 00: the limiter takes the thermal noise down more than it takes the leakage down, so the phase-noise residue and the ADC's noise, which ride on the leakage, stand higher against it than in a linear receiver
  at 20 dB of isolation less than 10 dB of SNR is left and the roads part by 3.5 dB: the closed form over-states the ADC's noise where nothing dithers it (example 00, section 5) and the simulation's peak search reads high at that SNR; neither figure is a design point
